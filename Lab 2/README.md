## Lab Overview
This lab demonstrated Agile project management principles using Atlassian Jira, translating the functional requirements of the *Smart Lab Equipment & Slot Reservation Portal* into Epics, User Stories, Story Points, Sprint Backlogs, and simulating Scrum board execution with Burndown chart analysis.

---

## Epics Structure
1. **SLESRP-1 — Equipment Reservation & Availability:** Support equipment discovery, reservation, cancellation, and reservation-history functions for students.
2. **SLESRP-2 — Equipment Calibration & Maintenance:** Manage equipment calibration status and provide calibration information needed to determine equipment suitability.
3. **SLESRP-3 — Late Return & Booking Penalties:** Detect late equipment returns and enforce the defined booking suspension rule.
4. **SLESRP-4 — Reservation Notifications:** Keep students informed about upcoming equipment reservations and reservation cancellations.
5. **SLESRP-5 — Lab Usage Reports & Analytics:** Provide lab technicians with usage reports, utilization statistics, and reservation analytics.
